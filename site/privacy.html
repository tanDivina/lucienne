Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Lucienne is a conversational website and SEO diagnostic copilot created by Divina Endeavors LLC doing business as Hero Apps (“we”, “us”, or “our”). This policy transparently outlines how data is handled when you interact with Lucienne or use our site.

Key Commitments:
  • We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data.
  • We do not retain permanent copies of scanned websites beyond your active session and audit briefing.
  • Submitted waitlist emails are used strictly for project milestone updates (e.g. texting channel rollout) and are never shared with third-party advertisers.

1. Information We Collect

  • Website URLs for Diagnosis: When you provide a website address, Lucienne performs public audits of publicly accessible assets (such as HTML meta tags, robots.txt, sitemaps, and Google PageSpeed metrics).
  • Contact Information: If you join our waitlist or request an emailed technical report, we collect your email address and optional website URL.
  • Conversation Logs: Interactive messages are processed in real time through Amazon Bedrock agents to generate helpful diagnostic responses.

2. How We Use Information

Data provided is used solely to:

  • Inspect and assess Core Web Vitals, technical SEO health, and speed metrics.
  • Dispatch requested technical briefings via Amazon Simple Email Service (SES).
  • Send waitlist updates regarding service availability.
  • Maintain service security, uptime, and abuse prevention.

3. Third-Party Infrastructure

Lucienne runs on enterprise cloud infrastructure adhering to strict security protocols:

  • Amazon Web Services (AWS): Compute (AWS Lambda), agent inference (Amazon Bedrock), messaging (AWS End User Messaging, Amazon SES), and secure session store (Amazon DynamoDB).
  • Vercel: Static frontend edge delivery and SSL termination.
  • Google PageSpeed API: Standard performance and Core Web Vitals diagnostic measurements.

4. Data Retention & Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

You have full rights under GDPR, CCPA, and applicable global privacy regulations to inspect, update, or request the deletion of any stored contact information. To exercise your rights, email us directly at support@hero-apps.com.
